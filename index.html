About

I'm Muhammad Kashif, a Media Studies student at Bahria University in Karachi with a growing focus on outreach, communication, and creative production. I currently work as PR and Partnerships Outreach Intern at Icon Training, an AI-powered fitness coaching startup, where I run outreach across investor, media, and athlete partnership tracks, writing personalised pitches that have opened real conversations with investors, journalists, and athletes.

Before this, I built experience across remote internships at Zwipe Marketplace, Fendsol, and GaoTek Inc, and co-founded BrandLift Media, a social media marketing agency offering video, static content, and page management. Alongside outreach work, I edit video, design motion graphics, and create visual content using Adobe Creative Suite and CapCut.

What ties all of this together is an interest in communication that actually connects, whether that's a pitch, a script, or a piece of content. Long term, I'm working toward Pakistan's civil service examinations, drawn to the space where media, policy, and public engagement meet.
